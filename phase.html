Interpretation

Dalí’s geometry: a tesseract has sixteen 4D vertices and thirty-two edges. Splitting Q4 by its fourth coordinate gives two eight-vertex cubes linked by eight edges.

Our sixth point: the *authorized, consequential relation* between bounded bubbles. It is not an extra vertex on a tesseract and does not add a physical dimension.
