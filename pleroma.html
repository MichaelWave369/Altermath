Two different layers of the project

Historical source

Names, groupings, syzygies and reported emanation parentage are recorded from Irenaeus, Against Heresies I.1, a polemical ancient source. Not all Gnostic traditions share this scheme.

Modern mechanism

Every source pair is preserved; pair genealogy is compiled into two communication edges per emanation, a deliberate modeling decision. Horos acts as a configurable access boundary.

Falsifiable experiment

Compare repair behavior, connectivity, and convergence under equal node and link budgets. The model can reveal topology effects without validating theology or additional physical dimensions.
