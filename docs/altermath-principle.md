# The Altermath Principle · v0.1

**Foundational proposition:** When two bounded, stateful systems establish a permitted connection across their boundaries, a *relational junction* can form. If that connection produces a measurable state or relationship change, an **Altermath event** has occurred.

> The sixth point is where one whole meets another and the interaction makes change possible.

## Five functions + one relation

1. Perception: acquire signals.
2. Memory: persist and retrieve state.
3. Computation: transform state.
4. Expression: produce outputs and actions.
5. Regulation: maintain constraints and permissions.
6. **Altermath:** a possibly transient, dynamic relationship enabling consequential contact.

The sixth point is not necessarily a fixed geometric center, another pentagram vertex, a universal consciousness, or a new physical dimension. A bubble may form many such junctions at once, with other bubbles inside or outside itself.

## Kinds of junction

- **Intra-bubble:** between a parent and child or two sibling domains.
- **Inter-bubble:** between independently bounded root systems.

A contact with no exchange is an eligible *junction*, not a completed *event* in this first operational model.

## Lifecycle

Eligibility → boundary authorization → contact → translation/exchange → measurable change → recorded trace.

In this simulator, channels stand in for a minimal form of translation; semantic interpretation and schema mediation are future work.

## What is established and what is proposed?

**Established tools:** graphs, diffusion, nested process hierarchies, boundary permissions, event traces and dynamic coupling.

**Proposed architecture:** a unified explicit notion of Altermath junctions with metrics and lifecycle that can be tested against ordinary message-passing designs.

**Symbolic inspirations:** the 5+1 human diagram, 3-6-9, carbon-12, nested-bubble imagery and Gnostic Pleroma. These are not evidence of new physical laws, hidden dimensional mechanisms, or consciousness.
